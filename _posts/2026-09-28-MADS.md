## Statistical Methods in Finance:

Finally, in Statistical Methods in Finance, I learned about each aspect of the Black-Scholes model. We covered the lognormal pricing model, nonparametric density estimation, the Ljung-Box test for autocorrelation, and volatility clustering models like ARCH, GARCH, and APARCH. In a Jupyter notebook, I ran a Shapiro-Wilk test and created Kernel density estimate plots and Normal probability plots to assess the Normality of weekly log returns compared to monthly log returns for a chosen company. I also created an autocorrelation plot and ran an augmented Dickey-Fuller test to assess the stationarity daily log returns for a selected firm.
